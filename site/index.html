Data feeds

The raw data behind this page, fetchable from anywhere (CORS is open):

  • events.json — JSON feed
  • events.ics — iCalendar feed

Google and Outlook don't have feed formats of their own — they subscribe to the iCalendar feed. The Add To Calendar button up top has one-click links for both.
