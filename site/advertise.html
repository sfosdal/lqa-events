Lower Queen Anne Events

Reach the people heading to Seattle Center, Climate Pledge Arena, McCaw Hall and the neighborhood’s bars and theatres — on the calendar they check before they go.

Who you reach

Lower Queen Anne Events is a free, ad-light calendar of everything on around Seattle Center: concerts, home games, theatre, festivals, movies and local bar nights, refreshed several times a day from the venues’ own listings. Its readers are locals and visitors planning an evening in the neighborhood — the audience a restaurant, bar, hotel, parking garage or rideshare promotion wants to meet an hour before showtime.

Placements

  • Presenting sponsor — a single line under the masthead on every page, with your mark and link.
  • Neighborhood spotlight — a card in the event list for your venue’s own nights, styled like the listings and marked as sponsored.
  • Game-day partner — your message on the Hometown Teams block during a home game, when the page is busiest.
  • Calendar feed — a short line in the subscribe feeds that readers add to their own calendars.

How it works

Placements are sold by the month, with a minimum of one month. Creative is a short line of text and a link, or a small mark; we keep the look of the site, so there are no banners, pop-ups or tracking beyond a click count. Rates depend on placement and season — the arena’s concert calendar and the home teams’ seasons set the traffic — and are quoted on request.
